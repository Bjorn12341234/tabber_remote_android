Tabber Spak is a remote control for Tabber Studio. It works only together with Tabber Studio running on your Mac.

Tabber Studio is a music app for the Mac, a DAW built for guitarists. Write in tab or notation, record audio and MIDI, use your plugins and mix the finished song. It works on its own, and you don't need Tabber Spak to use it. Tabber Spak lets you run it from your iPhone, with your instrument in your hands and the computer on the desk.

Record from across the room
- Play, pause and record on the armed tracks, with Count-in, the metronome and Loop.
- After each take, keep it, delete it (Undo brings it back) or go again from where it started.
- Every take is listed, and the newest one plays where takes overlap, as on the computer.

Tracks and tab
- Choose a track, hear it alone, mute it and arm it for recording.
- Add a tab, drum, MIDI or audio track, as Tabber Studio's own Add Track makes it.
- Write tab with a fretboard or a piano, on a tab track or as MIDI tab on a MIDI track, one undo step per note.
- Write drums on a drum track with Tabber Studio's Drum Map.

Mix
- Tabber Studio's own faders, pan, solo, mute and meters, for every track, bus and the master.
- Insert plug-ins from Tabber Studio's own list, bypass or remove them, or open a plug-in's window on the computer.
- Add a bus, choose where each track sends its sound, and see it all as Tabber Studio's Flow, where you can also wire it by hand.

Practise
- The Speed Trainer: loop a part slowly and let it speed up each time the loop comes round.
- A tuner that listens to your instrument.

Ask
- Ask Tabber Studio's chat for a change, typed or spoken. The phone shows the plan, and nothing changes until you tap Execute. The AI runs on your computer, with your own key.

Tabber Spak needs Tabber Studio 0.3.27 or later (0.3.29 for plug-ins, 0.3.31 for drums) on a Mac with Apple Silicon, free from tabber.tourbuddy.se. Pair the phone once by scanning the code the computer shows. It talks only to the computers you pair it with, over an encrypted connection on your own network. Tabber Spak collects no data.

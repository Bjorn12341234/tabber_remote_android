## Tabber Spak

Tabber Spak is the iPhone remote for Tabber Studio. It collects no data:
nothing about you or how you use it is sent to us or anyone else.

- **It talks only to your own computers.** Tabber Spak finds Tabber Studio on
  your network and talks only to the computers you pair it with, over an
  encrypted connection. Nothing it sends leaves your network.
- **Pairing stays on your devices.** Pairing makes a key on your iPhone, kept
  in its Keychain, and Tabber Studio remembers the phone on your Mac. Remove
  the phone in Tabber Studio's Preferences > Remote at any time.
- **The camera** is used only to scan the pairing code your computer shows.
- **The microphone** is used only by the tuner, which listens to your
  instrument. Nothing is recorded, and the sound never leaves your iPhone.
- **Face ID or your passcode** opens Chat, because Chat can change your song.
  Tabber Spak never sees your face data.
- **Chat** sends what you type or say to Tabber Studio on your computer. The
  AI runs there, with the AI provider and key you set up in Tabber Studio;
  your phone never holds the key.
- **No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.**
